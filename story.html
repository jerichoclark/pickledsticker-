Our story

Every cup gives $1 back to kids.

Pickled Sticker tumblers are handmade one at a time, and each one helps kids in our own community.

$1

Our main goal 💛

For every item sold, $1 goes straight back to kids. It's split between two causes close to our hearts:

🧒 Local kids in need

Part of every dollar helps children in our area who are going without. Think school supplies, warm clothes, and the everyday basics every kid deserves.

📚 Homeschool families

The rest goes to local homeschool families for extracurricular supplies and field trips: art kits, science gear, museum visits and days out that make learning fun.

🔁 It adds up

One tumbler is $1. A bridal party of eight is $8. A whole team order is even more. Every cup you buy makes a real difference.

How your tumbler is made 🎨

From your click to your doorstep, here's what happens.

  1. You pick your cup. Shop the Mom, Dad or Custom pages and add a name or phrase if you'd like it personalized.
  2. You check out safely. Payment goes through PayPal, so your card details never touch our site.
  3. We make it by hand. Your design is printed and heat-pressed with sublimation, which bakes the ink right into the coating so it won't peel, crack or fade.
  4. We check and pack it. Every tumbler is inspected and packed with care, ready to gift.
  5. It ships to you. Most orders ship within 3 to 5 business days.
  6. $1 goes to kids. Your order adds a dollar to our giving for local children and homeschool families.

Who we are 👋

We're a husband and wife team who fell in love with making tumblers. Every cup is pressed, checked and packed by us, in small batches, so you get something made with real care. Pickled Sticker started as a fun side project and grew into a way to give back to the kids around us.
